Help — Running Concrete Buddy

Running Concrete Buddy

It works the way your hands already expect: type a number, tell it what the number is — feet, inches, or a fraction — and it keeps everything in feet-inch-fraction for you. Every key this pad shares with Framer Buddy works exactly the way it works in Framer.

Buddy Help Desk

Questions, problems, or an idea for the app? Tap the button and your email app opens, already addressed to the Buddy Help Desk. A real person answers.

Enter a measurement

For 5 feet, 3 and a half inches: tap 5 Feet 3 Inch 1 ⁄ 2. Add, subtract, multiply and divide measurements just like plain numbers — answers come back in feet, inches and fractions. Multiply one measurement by another and the answer is an area instead: it reads in square feet, with square inches and square yards on the line underneath — and dividing that area by a length gives the other length back. Tap ⌫ to back up one step, and On/C once to clear the entry, twice to clear the whole calculation.

Fraction resolution & display

Tap Fraction res in the right-hand column to cycle how fine fractions read out: 1/2, 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32, 1/64. Tap Disp under it to cycle the answer display between feet-inch-fraction, decimal feet and decimal inches.

Rise, Run & Diag — the triangle

Enter the rise and tap Rise, enter the run and tap Run. Then tap Diag for the third side — give it any two sides and it works out the one that's missing: rise 3 ft and run 4 ft hands back a 5 ft diagonal. The three sides are kept on the STORED page.

Convert — the yellow key

Tap Conv, then any unit key — yds, Feet, Inch, m, cm or mm — to see the measurement on screen in that unit, imperial or metric: lengths convert as lengths, areas as areas, and volumes and pour totals as volumes. Arm Conv again and tap another unit key to convert again, back and forth as often as you like; with Conv lit, ⁄ flips between fraction and decimal display.

Memory — Store, Rcl & Mem+

Store saves the number on screen to memory, Mem+ adds the number on screen to memory, and Rcl brings it back. While memory holds a value the MEM flag lights at the top of the display, and the value itself is listed on the STORED page. Beside the memory keys in the top row sit Columns and Tubes.

Columns — the guided pour

Tap Columns and the display walks you through it — prompt text on top, your numbers underneath. Enter Width In FT-In-Frac- Press Enter, then Depth, then Height the same way: type each measurement with Feet, Inch and ⁄, and tap Enter after each one. That column's volume is worked out and banked in the background. At If Finished Press Enter, To Add Column Press Add, tap Add to run the next column the same way, or Enter when the run is done. At the last prompt, Add shows the running count — every column banked, totalled in cubic yards — Store saves that count on the STORED page, and On/C exits. Made a mistake? ⌫ deletes a digit while you type, and with nothing typed it steps back one prompt with your numbers restored — stepping back from the Finished prompt un-banks that last column, so it is never counted twice. The back instruction only appears in the prompt while there is a step to go back to. On/C clears a half-typed entry; pressed with nothing typed, it exits Columns, and a run that was never stored is dropped.

Slabs — the guided pour

Tap Slabs and the display walks you through it — prompt text on top, your numbers underneath. Enter Slab Length In FT-In-Frac- Press Enter, then Slab Width, then Slab Thickness the same way: type each measurement with Feet, Inch and ⁄, and tap Enter after each one. The flat part of the slab is worked out and held. Then come the club prompts — Enter Club Height In FT-In-Frac- Press Enter, then Club Width. The club height is the full thickness at the edge, the slab itself included — a 12-inch club on a 6-inch slab — and the app adds only the part below the slab. You never type the club's length: the app runs it around the slab's own perimeter, twice the length plus twice the width, and adds it into the same slab. Then it asks Interior Footings? — tap Add to run an interior footing strip (Width, Thickness, Lineal Distance — the thickness is the total at the strip, slab included, and the app again adds only the part below the slab), or Enter if there are none; after each strip it asks Additional Interior Footings? until you press Enter. At If Finished Press Enter, To Add Slab Press Add, tap Add to run the next slab the same way, or Enter when the run is done. At the last prompt, Add shows the running count — every slab banked, totalled in cubic yards — Store saves that count on the STORED page, and On/C exits. Made a mistake? ⌫ deletes a digit while you type, and with nothing typed it steps back one prompt with your numbers restored — stepping back from the Finished prompt un-banks that last slab and returns you to its last measurement — the last interior strip's Lineal Distance, or the Club Width when there are no strips — so it is never counted twice. The back instruction only appears in the prompt while there is a step to go back to. On/C clears a half-typed entry; pressed with nothing typed, it exits Slabs, and a run that was never stored is dropped.

Mesh — the sheet count

Tap Mesh and the display walks you through it — prompt text on top, your numbers underneath. Enter Area Length In FT-In-Frac- Press Enter, then Area Width the same way: that is the square footage you are covering. Then the sheet itself — Enter Sheet Width In FT-In-Frac- Press Enter, then Sheet Length — because sheets come in various sizes, the app measures the sheet you are actually using. It divides the area by the sheet's coverage and banks the count, rounded up to whole sheets — you can't order part of a sheet. At If Finished Press Enter, To Add Mesh Press Add, tap Add to run another area the same way, or Enter when the run is done. At the last prompt, Add shows the running count — every area banked, totalled in sheets — Store saves that count on the STORED page, and On/C exits. Mesh stands on its own: no slab, pad or saved page needed — run it on a blank page and it just hands you a sheet count. While a saved page is open as a file, Store adds the mesh count to that page and files the page on the phone right away, with its name still at the top and no reminder — the mesh belongs to that job, so it is saved with it. Sheets are a count, not a measurement, so Conv never converts them. Made a mistake? ⌫ deletes a digit while you type, and with nothing typed it steps back one prompt with your numbers restored — stepping back from the Finished prompt un-banks that last area and returns you to its Sheet Length, so it is never counted twice. The back instruction only appears in the prompt while there is a step to go back to. On/C clears a half-typed entry; pressed with nothing typed, it exits Mesh, and a run that was never stored is dropped.

Rebar — the count

Tap Rebar and the display walks you through it — prompt text on top, your numbers underneath. Enter Number Of Rows - Press Enter first: how many rows of bar the piece carries — two, four, six, whatever it takes — typed as a plain count with the number keys. Then Enter Lineal Distance In FT-In-Frac- Press Enter for the run itself, and Enter Stirrup Spacing In FT-In-Frac- Press Enter If None — the centers the stirrups run at on that lineal, 16, 24, 36, 48, whatever the job calls for. Every lineal is asked for its own spacing, so one leg can run at 24-inch centers and the next at 12. A lineal that carries no stirrups at all — steel for a wall — just takes Enter at the spacing prompt with nothing typed, and it banks as bar alone, with no stirrup count on its line. The stirrups for each lineal are figured from its length at its spacing, like studs in a wall. At Additional Lineal Distance?, tap Add to run another lineal the same way — its length times the rows adds into the same piece — or Enter when the piece is done and it banks: the bar reads in lineal feet, rows times every lineal added together, with the stirrup count beside it, ready to order. At If Finished Press Enter, To Add Rebar Press Add, tap Add to start the next piece — it asks the rows again — or Enter when the run is done. At the last prompt, Add shows the running count, Store saves that count on the STORED page, and On/C exits. The bar total is a length: with a total on screen, Conv and a unit key show it in metres, centimetres or millimetres, and Feet brings it back to feet — the stirrup count is a count and never converts. Made a mistake? ⌫ deletes a digit while you type, and with nothing typed it steps back one prompt with your numbers restored — stepping back from the Finished prompt un-banks that last piece and returns you to its last lineal's Stirrup Spacing, so it is never counted twice. The back instruction only appears in the prompt while there is a step to go back to. On/C clears a half-typed entry; pressed with nothing typed, it exits Rebar, and a run that was never stored is dropped.

Every key is live

Every key on this pad has its function now — Rebar was the last of Concrete's own keys to get one. Outside the Columns, Footings, Walls, Pads, Slabs, Mesh, Tubes and Rebar sequences, Enter in the Slabs column works exactly like = — it finishes the calculation on screen; inside a sequence, Enter steps you through the prompts.

What's stored

Tap STORED at the top any time to see the Rise / Run / Diag triangle, the Memory value and any Columns, Footings, Walls, Pads, Slabs, Mesh, Tubes or Rebar running count you saved with Store, held on the job. In that window, Clear triangle wipes the three sides, ready for the next one, and Clear columns, Clear footings, Clear walls, Clear pads, Clear slabs, Clear mesh, Clear tubes and Clear rebar each wipe a saved running count. At the bottom of that page, Send by email opens your email app with the whole stored page filled in — just add who it’s going to and hit Send. Export PDF and Export spreadsheet send the whole stored page out as a file instead — exporting opens your share sheet, so you can email the file or send it to another app; where sharing isn’t offered, the file downloads instead. The page also keeps copies on this phone. Every Store automatically keeps the latest page on the phone as the Auto-Saved page. Save To Phone files the page as it stands as its own permanent saved page under a name — the date and contents are filled in for you, or type your own — and you can save as many pages as you like. Tap Retrieve From Phone to open the Saved Pages list, newest first, each page with its name, when it was saved and its totals; tap one to bring it back onto this page, with its name at the top so you know which page you’re looking at. A page you bring back is open like a file — its name stays at the top while you work on it. Tap On/C on the calculator to close the open page — the file closes, but everything on this page stays right where it is. Store a new running count for a pour that is already on the page and the new entries join the ones already there — one count, old and new together; clear a pour first and the next Store for it starts a fresh count instead. While a page is open, the calculator shows its name at the top of the display; change the opened page and that line turns into a yellow reminder naming the page until you file it again with Save To Phone — its name is filled in for you, so re-filing it is one tap, or type a different name to save it as a new page. Press and hold a page in the list to open its options — recall it from there, or delete it from the phone, for culling pages you no longer need. The Clear buttons clear only what you see on this page — they never remove a saved page from the phone, and they never touch the calculation on the calculator’s display.

Licensing — your key

Concrete Buddy activates once with the license key that comes with your purchase — it is in your purchase email. The first time the app asks, type or paste the key in and tap Activate; that one check needs the internet. From then on the app is yours on this device and works fully offline — no account, no sign-in, nothing phones home. A copy shared to another phone wakes up unactivated: its number keys stay dead and its display says so, and a key stops working once it has been activated on its allowed number of devices. Lost your key, or hit the device limit? Contact the Buddy Help Desk and it will be sorted out.

Browser — Suppliers & Lookup

Tap Browser and this window is a door to websites — nothing else. It opens real sites, never a search about a site.
Project Buddy Professional Services Homepage sits at the very top and opens the Project Buddy website home page. It is locked in place — it cannot be removed — and it stays tappable like every other link.
Google sits under it and opens Google itself — your fallback for finding anything.
Home Depot sits right under Google and the button says only that. It opens Home Depot’s own home page — the Canadian home page for a Canadian postal code, the US home page for a US ZIP — using the postal code or ZIP at the top of the window. Enter it once (or tap Use my location) and it’s remembered on this device. It never opens a Google search about Home Depot.
Your own suppliers: tap the yellow Add Your Supplier Url button. Find your supplier on Google first, copy their website address right from there, and paste it in — no typing long addresses from memory. Then name the button yourself, the same look as the Home Depot button, and tap Add supplier. Your yard, your concrete plant, whoever you actually buy from — the list is saved on this device and each button opens that supplier’s own website.
Taking one off: every supplier you add carries a small Clear button beside it — one tap and it’s off the shelf. The Project Buddy homepage button, Google and Home Depot are fixed and stay put.
These links open live pages, so they need data; the calculator itself never does.
