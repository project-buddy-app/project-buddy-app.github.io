About

45 years on the tools. The knowledge isn’t done.

👷
Dave — Thunder Bay, OntarioCarpenter • 45 years’ experience

I’m Dave, a carpenter from Thunder Bay with 45 years in the trade. My body doesn’t like construction work the way it used to — but everything those years taught me still works fine, and it seemed a waste to let it sit idle.

So I’m building it into tools instead. Every Buddy starts from how the work is actually done on site, gets tested against real numbers and the code book, and only ships when it holds up. If a feature could cost a user more trust than it earns, it doesn’t ship — that rule has already cut features from this lineup, and it’ll cut more.

The calculators come first and they stay honest: tested math does the precision work, and nothing here is ever sold as a subscription. You buy it, it’s yours.
