A word before the listings. This guide is laid out the way the old paperback was — a log, in time order, the way it ran for fifty years. One change, made on purpose: there are no channel numbers in this book. Each listing opens with the network's name in the black bullet where the number used to sit, because nobody hunts by numbers anymore. Local stations wear red. Everything is Eastern time, Thunder Bay — your time, your town.

Notes, News & Nonsense17

TV Teletype

STILL WANDERING DEPT. — The Littlest Hobo turned up on CKPR Sunday at 8:30 in the morning, in "The Genesis Tapes," trying to recover videotapes that would expose his true identity. The dog has been on the road since 1979 and has never once been adopted. At this point it is clearly a lifestyle choice.

11:29, NOT 11:30 — Saturday Night Live begins at 11:29 PM on CKPR this Saturday, as it has for fifty years. If your clock disagrees, your clock is wrong, not the show.

COIN-FLIP DEPT. — Wednesday's Survivor (Global, 8:00) features a million-dollar coin flip revealed at Tribal Council. A coin flip. For a million dollars. The producers have stopped pretending otherwise, and honestly — respect.

CIVIC PRIDE DEPT. — Thunder Bay's city council has a podcast, "The Minutes," exploring the details of council meetings, and CKPR airs it on Saturday mornings. Appointment listening, for exactly the people who run this town.

'TIL DEATH DEPT. — Eternally Yours (Global, Thursdays) follows a vampire couple whose marriage has lasted 500 years and gone, in the listing's own word, "pulseless." Five centuries, and nobody suggested counselling until now.

ACROSS THE POND DEPT. — Saturday Night Live now has a British edition — SNL UK, early Saturdays on CKPR, Jack Whitehall hosting this week. Same sketches. More polite apologies.

THE MAILBAG — Empty. It's our first week. Letters to the editor may be delivered in person, across the room, at any hour. The editor is listening.
