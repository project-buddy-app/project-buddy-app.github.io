Framer Buddy

The full framing calculator — studs, sheets and rafters in feet-inch-fraction, with running totals.

The one that started it all, modeled on the calculator every contractor already knows — then taken further. It works the way your hands expect: type a number, tell it feet, inches or a fraction, and it stays in feet-inch-fraction for you.

  • Studs & sheets: counts at 16″ or 24″ centres with running totals, wall after wall, area after area
  • Common, hip & jack rafters — pitch / rise / run / diagonal triangle solving
  • Stored on the job: kept lists and totals, export to PDF or spreadsheet, or send by email
  • Circle calcs, %, √, x², Store / Rcl / M+
  • Yellow Conv key: yards, metres, cm, mm, decimal feet
  • Fraction resolution 1/2 through 1/64
  • No ads, no bloatware, no subscriptions — a clean, straight, safe app
  • Safe to install: Android Play Protect scans the app on your phone — that scan is there to protect you, and it's how you know the app is clean

Built and tested — a full trade calculator.

Roofer Buddy

Pitches, rafter lengths, bundles, hips & valleys — roofing math without the guesswork.

Roofing quantities and cuts from the same triangle brain as Framer Buddy, aimed at the roof: pitch to rafter, area to bundles, with waste figured the way roofers actually order. Shingles, ice & water shield and metal roofing totals, all three systems side by side.

  • Pitch key: /12, degrees, slope % and slope factor
  • Common, hip and jack rafters — overhang tail and ridge deduction shown separately
  • Roof totals: squares, bundles, underlayment rolls, ridge cap, drip edge
  • Ice & water shield: eave courses and valleys, in 66 ft rolls
  • Metal roofing: panel counts at 36″ or 16″ coverage, screws, closures, ridge
  • Adjustable waste, 10% to start

Built and tested — a full trade calculator.

Concrete Buddy

Slabs, footings, foundations and walls — volume, bags and a full rebar takeoff in one place.

Yards for the truck, bags for the small jobs, and the gravel base under both. Built for pads, footings, foundation walls and tubes — volume with waste figured in, and an order sheet that rounds up the way the yard does. Foundations live here too: footing runs figured from the building size, formwork and rebar on the same screen.

  • Slab, footing, wall, pad and tube volumes (ft³ / yd³ / m³)
  • Building length × width figures the footing perimeter run
  • Rebar takeoff — bar counts by size, laps figured, total weight
  • Formwork contact area & anchor bolts
  • Bag counts for small pours — 30 kg, 80 lb and 60 lb, rounded up
  • Gravel base volume, figured separately
  • Totals order sheet: every pour listed, grand total, order quantity rounded up to the nearest ½ m³
  • Results stored on the job, on your device

Built and tested — a full trade calculator.

Built into every single

🌐 A supplier browser in every Buddy

Every single carries a built-in browser for looking things up without leaving the job — store product pages, today’s prices, code and span references. It is location-based: set your town once, and the searches and supplier links come up local to you — your stores, your prices. A guy in Calgary gets Calgary results, not some other city’s. The calculators themselves work fully offline; the browser just uses your data connection when you have one.
