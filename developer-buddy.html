How it works

  • 1. Tell us the problem. One email, in your own words. What do you do on paper or in a spreadsheet that eats your time?
  • 2. You get a written spec and a fixed price. What the app will do, in plain words, and exactly what it costs. No hourly meter running, ever.
  • 3. A deposit starts the build. Half up front, half on delivery. Nothing begins without the spec you approved.
  • 4. You test it like family. You get the app early and a direct line to the developer — tell us what fits wrong, and we fix it before it's finished.
  • 5. Delivery. You get the finished app, ready to use in your business. Support comes from the same people who built it, not a call centre.

The rules — in plain words

  • Fixed price. The price in the spec is the price. Full stop.
  • Changes are change orders. Want something added mid-build? It's written up and priced before the work, never after — same as any honest renovation.
  • One build at a time. We take a limited number of projects so every build gets finished properly. If the bench is full, we'll tell you when it opens.
  • The app stays our property. You pay for the build, and it's yours to use in your business — no subscription, no renting it month to month, no hostage games. But like every product we make, the app itself remains Project Buddy property, and we may offer it to others.
  • Want to own it outright? That exists — it's the second way to buy, below, and it's priced like it. Your call, in writing, before we start.
  • Straight answers. If we can't build what you need — or shouldn't — we'll tell you that instead of taking your deposit.

What does it cost? Two ways to buy

1. Build & use — the regular price. We build it, you use it in your business for as long as you like. The app stays Project Buddy property, and we may offer it to others. This is the affordable way to get exactly the tool you need.

2. Own it outright — the buyout price. The ownership rights transfer to you. It costs a great deal more, because you're buying the product, not just the build. And if you'd like it sold through our stores afterwards, we can market it for you for a cut — your ownership, our shelf.

Every build is quoted individually, after we've looked at the job — the same way you'd quote work yourself. The quote is free, in writing, and it's fixed.
